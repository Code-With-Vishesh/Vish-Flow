One initiative, many ways in

Vish Flow is an internship initiative from Code With Vishesh for students and self-learners who want to practise real skills on real projects. You pick a domain and a level; the work is shaped around where you are now.

Learn

Follow a structured plan for your domain, from fundamentals to deeper topics.

Build

Turn what you learn into projects you can explain and show.

Grow

Reflect on feedback, tackle harder tasks, and move up a level.

One program, three skill levels

Every domain follows the same three pathways. Requirements and assignments vary by domain and level.

Beginner

Fundamentals and guided projects. Good if you are new to the domain.

Intermediate

Independent implementation and integrations. Good if you can build small things unaided.

Advanced

Complex projects and deeper technical problem-solving.

Example projects

Illustrative project ideas showing the kind of work each level could involve. They are not completed intern work.

How it works

The proposed path from first look to final feedback.

  1. Explore a domain

    Browse domains and pick a level that fits you.

  2. Submit an application

    Fill in the official Google Form.

  3. Application review and selection

    Applications are reviewed. Submitting does not guarantee selection.

  4. Orientation and learning plan

    Selected applicants receive their plan.

  5. Complete assignments and projects

    Work through tasks for your domain and level.

  6. Final evaluation and feedback

    Your work is reviewed and you get feedback.

This workflow is a proposal. Dates, duration, and operating details will be published once confirmed.

What you can expect to gain

Project-based learning

Learn by building, not only by watching.

Portfolio development

Finish with work you can show and explain.

Practical skills

Practise tools and workflows used in real projects.

Structured tasks

Clear assignments matched to your level.

Mentor feedback

Feedback on your work, where mentors are available for your domain.

Vish Flow does not promise jobs, stipends, placement, or guaranteed selection.

Community and mentorship

The plan is for participants to learn alongside peers and get feedback from mentors in their domain. Mentor names and group formats will be shared once confirmed.
